Privacy Policy for GoldAlert

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the GoldAlert mobile application (the "App") provided as a Free service. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

The GoldAlert App does not collect, store, or share any personal information or sensitive user data. All data related to your price thresholds, alerts, and application settings is stored strictly locally on your device using a secure, encrypted database.

Internet Access

The App requires an internet connection solely for the purpose of fetching real-time gold prices from a remote server. No personal identifiers, tracking data, or device metrics are transmitted during this process.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party data collection services. We do not use third-party analytics (such as Firebase Analytics) or advertising frameworks (such as Google AdMob) that may track or identify you.

Security

We value your privacy and security. Your local application data is protected using SQLCipher encryption, ensuring that your custom threshold configurations and application state remain private and secure on your physical device.

Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since the App collects no personal information whatsoever, no children's data is at risk.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the email address provided in the Google Play Store listing.
